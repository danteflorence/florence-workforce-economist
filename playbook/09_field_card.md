# One-page field card

> _Everything you need, on one page. Open this before any call. It is
> deliberately short. Use it as memory, not as a script._

---

## What we are
Florence places **permanent international RNs** at US healthcare
operators. The pricing engine quotes savings facility-by-facility from
public BLS, CMS, and NASHP data.

## What we sell
**Savings + capacity, not staffing.**
Same hours, same coverage, lower cost, higher retention.

## The economics — by care setting

**Inpatient (hospitals):**
- Placement fee: **$50K per RN**, amortized over 36 months ≈
  **$1,389/RN/month**
- Replaces premium agency rates: **$150-$200/hr** today
- Annual savings example: **$60-$120K/RN** at typical agency premium
- Pitch: "Savings + capacity"

**Outpatient (ASCs, HHAs, SNFs, hospice, dialysis):**
- Monthly subscription: **~$750-$2,000/RN/month** (locally calibrated)
- One-month deposit at signing → 24-month term → applied to last month
- Customer pays on credit card every month — no $50K upfront
- Pitch: "Revenue capacity unlocked, monthly cost on the opex line"

**Both settings:** retention is **multiples** of agency tenure.

## The pitch in three moves
1. **Name the pain.** "You're paying premium agency rates for a
   problem that isn't fixing itself."
2. **Name the unlock.** "We turn that premium into permanent capacity,
   priced facility by facility from your own cost report."
3. **Make the ask.** "20 minutes to walk your numbers."

## What to lead with — by persona
| Persona | Open with |
|---------|-----------|
| CFO     | "The Excel has every facility broken out with the math shown." |
| CNO     | "Let me show you the nurses first, then the numbers." |
| TA / HR | "We own visa + credentialing. You own orientation." |
| COO     | "What service line would you grow with 10 more permanent RNs?" |

## What NOT to say
- "FICA" / "tax exempt" — internal only
- "Visa class" / "OPT" — internal only
- "We can do <N> nurses next year" — sounds aggressive; offer a cohort
- "Trust us, the savings are real" — show them the Excel instead

## When the data is challenged
1. Open the **Data provenance** tab
2. Cite the source row explicitly ("FY23 HCRIS Worksheet S-3 Part V")
3. Acknowledge the lag (12-18 months)
4. Offer the data-share upgrade

## Deal stages
**Prospect → Discovery → Proposal → Review → Closed**
- Don't generate a proposal until discovery is logged.
- Don't negotiate without sales-leadership review for anything below
  Reference tier.

## When to bring leadership in
- Price below the Reference band
- Non-standard contract terms
- A commitment to a specific delivery date
- ANY hint of union, regulatory, or compliance complexity

## Top objections — your 3-second answer
- **"We've tried international before"** → cohort model, not one-offs
- **"Union concerns"** → permanent FTE, prevailing wage, displaces
  agency, not members
- **"Internal recruiting prefers in-house"** → make TA the hero; we
  expand their story
- **"Data is from 2023"** → upgrade to data-share partnership
- **"Fee is too high"** → ask what outcome they're trying to buy
- **"Hiring freeze"** → start on retainer; freeze lifts in 6-9 mo
  anyway

## Sources we cite, on demand
- **BLS** OEWS (wages), JOLTS (openings/hires/quits), CES (employment)
- **CMS** HCRIS Worksheet S-3 Part V (contract labor), Care Compare
- **NASHP** Hospital Cost Tool (ownership, financials)
- **Florence cohort data** (retention, time-to-placement)

## How long things take
- Prospect → Discovery: 2-4 weeks
- Discovery → Proposal: 1-2 weeks
- Proposal → Close: 4-12 weeks
- Contract → Nurse on floor: 6-9 months

## The two numbers to memorize
**Inpatient: ~$1,389/RN/month, amortized $50K placement fee.**
**Outpatient: ~$750-$2,000/RN/month, credit-card subscription with 1-month deposit.**
Everything else flows from there.
