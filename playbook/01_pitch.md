## The 60-second pitch

US hospitals are paying premium agency rates for registered nurses — often
$150 to $200 per hour, all-in — for a problem that isn't going to fix
itself. The shortage is structural, not cyclical: the Bureau of Labor
Statistics projects healthcare openings will outrun hires every year
through 2030.

Florence converts that premium agency spend into **permanent capacity**.
We source, credential, and place internationally-trained registered
nurses into US health systems on a permanent basis. The hospital pays a
one-time placement fee — typically about $50,000 per nurse, amortized
over 36 months. In exchange, they keep the nurse for years, not days; they
stop competing for agency staffing weekly; and they reclaim the gap
between what they pay an agency and what a permanent RN actually costs.

For a hospital running <X_AGENCY_FTE> agency RNs at <Y_RATE>/hr today,
that's about **<Z_DOLLARS> per year reclaimed**, without changing a single
hour of patient coverage.

Florence is the only platform that prices this savings locally — facility
by facility, using public BLS wage data, CMS cost reports, and our own
placement history — so the number we put in front of a CFO is defensible
on the first call.

**The ask in the first meeting:** 20 minutes to walk through the savings
analysis for two or three of their facilities. That's all.

## The 5-minute explainer

### What we are

Florence is a **workforce intelligence and placement platform** for US
healthcare. We do two things, both at once:

1. **Place permanent, internationally-trained RNs** into US health
   systems — credentialed, NCLEX-passed, English-proficient, ready to
   work in acute or non-acute settings.
2. **Quantify the economics** of doing that, hospital by hospital, using
   live data from BLS, CMS, NASHP, and our own production cohort.

The placement business is the revenue; the pricing engine is the moat.
The reason a CFO takes us seriously on the first call is that we hand
them a number that already accounts for their specific contract-labor
mix, their MSA wage, and their facility's own cost report.

### What we're not

- We are **not a staffing agency.** We do not run a per-shift book of
  business. We place permanent RNs.
- We are **not a recruiting firm.** We don't take a commission and
  disappear. We follow the nurse and the operator for at least 24 months
  through our portal.
- We are **not a visa-only solution.** The visa pathway is one piece. The
  pricing engine, the production cohort, and the nurse portal are equally
  important.

### Why now

Three trends collide in 2026:

1. **The agency rate ceiling is breaking.** Hospitals that locked in
   $180/hr crisis-era rates are watching them fall slowly — but not far
   enough or fast enough to fix their margins.
2. **CMS Worksheet S-3 Part V** now requires hospitals to report contract
   labor hours and dollars by line. For the first time we can read the
   pain quantitatively at the facility level.
3. **The international RN pipeline is healthier than it has been since
   2019.** Our visa-pathway cohorts are clearing credentialing in
   shorter windows.

### Why us

- **We sell the savings, not the staffing.** The customer's CFO is the
  primary buyer; the CNO is the validator. The numbers come first.
- **The nurse is the product, not the throughput.** Our portal gives
  every placed RN a career, a community, and a wage-benchmarking layer
  that no agency has. Retention follows.
- **The pricing engine is the differentiator.** Any competitor can quote
  $50K. Only we can show a hospital what their actual annual savings will
  be, by facility, with provenance for every input.
