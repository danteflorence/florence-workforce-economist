Methodology

Florence Workforce Restoration Economics v2 (May 28, 2026). Florence Monthly Fee per RN = Suggested Fee, clamped to floor/ceiling, where Suggested Fee = Employer FICA Savings ÷ 50% target. Employer FICA savings calculated under IRC §3121(b)(19) for F-1 nonresident-alien RNs during their nonresident-alien tax period. Guardrails: $750 ≤ fee ≤ $2,000.

Data sources

Hospital roster: CMS Hospital General Information (data.cms.gov). Per-hospital salaries, FTE, contract labor: CMS HCRIS Hospital Provider Cost Report 2023. RN wage benchmarks: BLS OEWS state-level. Agency rate benchmarks: hybrid (customer-disclosed where available, CommonSpirit anchor, state median).

Required compliance statement: Estimated employer-side FICA offset shown for eligible nurse cohorts only. Eligibility must be validated by payroll, tax counsel, and immigration counsel. Employee-side FICA benefit is shown separately as a nurse take-home benefit and is not included in employer ROI unless presented as combined economic value.
IRS sources: Foreign Student Liability for Social Security and Medicare Taxes ; IRC §3121(b)(19); IRS Publication 519; IRS Publication 15 (Circular E). Calibration: v0.5-methodology-v2-2026-05 · Pricing mode: fica_offset_target · Florence does not provide tax, payroll, immigration, or legal advice.
